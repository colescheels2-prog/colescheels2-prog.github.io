Lumis OS

A personal desktop assistant, built and used by Cole Scheels on his own Windows computer.

Lumis OS is not a public product and has no other users. It runs on one machine and helps its owner with his own day: his calendar, his inbox, his training log, and his own projects.

What it connects to

  • Google Calendar: it reads the owner's primary calendar so it can answer "what's on tomorrow", and creates, updates or removes events when he asks it to.
  • Gmail: it reads and searches his inbox, marks, labels, archives or trashes messages when he asks, and writes drafts for him to review.

Access is granted by the owner through Google's own consent screen and can be revoked at any time from Google Account → Third-party access.
