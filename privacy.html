Privacy Policy

Lumis OS · effective 26 September 2026

Lumis OS is a personal desktop assistant built and used by Cole Scheels on his own computer. It has a single user, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else. This policy explains what Google user data it accesses, why, and what happens to it.

Google data it accesses

  • Gmail (gmail.modify): reading and searching messages and threads; marking messages read, labelling, archiving or moving them to trash when the owner asks; creating drafts; and sending a message only when the owner asks it to. It never permanently deletes mail.
  • Google Calendar (calendar): reading events on the owner's primary calendar; creating, updating or deleting events when he asks.

How it uses that data

Only to do what the owner asks of his own assistant: answer questions about his schedule and inbox, draft replies, keep his calendar up to date, and remind him of what matters. The data is not used for advertising, is not sold, and is not used to build profiles of anyone other than the owner.

To answer a request, the relevant email or calendar text may be sent, as part of that request, to the AI model provider that generates the reply (currently models reached through OpenRouter, Anthropic and xAI). It is sent only to produce that reply for the owner.

Google Workspace APIs are not used to develop, improve, or train non-personalized AI and/or ML models. Gmail and Calendar data obtained through them is never used to train any AI or machine-learning model.

Where it is stored

Google access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer, encrypted with Windows' own per-user data protection. Summaries of recent mail and calendar events are kept in local files on that computer so the assistant can recall them. Nothing is stored on a server operated for Lumis OS; there is none.

Sharing

Google user data is not shared, transferred or disclosed to anyone except the AI model providers named above, and only as described above, or where required by law.

Lumis OS's use of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The use of information received from Google Workspace scopes will adhere to the Google User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How it is protected

Lumis OS runs only on its owner's password-protected Windows account. Google tokens are encrypted at rest with Windows' per-user data protection, all traffic to Google and to the model providers uses HTTPS, and no Google data is exposed to any network service run for Lumis OS.

Retention

Local summaries of mail and calendar events are kept only as long as they are useful to the owner and are replaced as the mirrors refresh; Gmail and Calendar themselves remain the record. Revoking access (below) stops all further access immediately.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account → Third-party access, or by disconnecting Google in Lumis OS's settings, which deletes the stored tokens. The local summaries can be deleted from the computer at any time.
